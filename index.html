02Approach

We work to industry standards — and often past them. Precision is not an aesthetic; it’s a requirement of the systems we build.

Healthcare and infrastructure share a quiet demand: they must simply work, every time, for a very long time.

So we design with the constraints on the table — regulatory, thermal, electrical, clinical — and treat them as the starting geometry of the solution, not obstacles to route around.

The result is work that survives contact with the real world.

/ 01

First-principles engineering

We prefer to understand a system deeply before we decide what to build, and we’re willing to go down to physics when it matters.

/ 02

Standards, not shortcuts

UL, ISO, IEC, FHIR, NEC — the right standard at the right layer. It’s how we ship work that’s auditable, interoperable and durable.

/ 03

Built, not briefed

We run our own ventures in the same domains we serve. The result is a team that thinks like operators, not advisors.

/ 04

Design as discipline

Aesthetic, mechanical, electrical, software — all one surface. We resist the handoff culture that produces fragmented products.
